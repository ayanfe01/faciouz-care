About Faciouz Care

A restaurant built on love, culture, and the flavours of Nigeria

Our Story

Where It All Began

Faciouz Care was founded with one simple mission: to bring the warmth and richness of Nigerian home cooking to a wider audience. What started as a passion project grew into a beloved dining destination where guests are treated like family from the moment they walk through our doors.

Our menu is a celebration of Nigeria's diverse culinary heritage — from the earthy depth of Amala and Ewedu to the smoky, festive energy of Party Jollof Rice. Every recipe tells a story, and every plate carries the pride of our culture.

We believe food is more than sustenance — it is connection, memory, and identity. At Faciouz Care, we honour that belief every single day by cooking with integrity, fresh ingredients, and a whole lot of heart.
